Arrival

We are at 15th Mile, Hallan-i, Manali, Himachal Pradesh 175136. Share your arrival time and we guide you in. Check-in is from 12 PM and check-out is at 11 AM the next day. You are welcome to arrive at any time of day, with no extra charge. Mountain roads and weather can slow you down, so plan some buffer.

Your stay

  • Each room has a double bed and hosts two guests. A third guest fits on an extra mattress, ask us.
  • A cleaner and a cook are available. Tell us what you need.
  • Dogs live on site. Guest pets, children and unmarried couples are welcome, with no limit. Alcohol is accepted.
  • Free laundry with our washing machine.
  • Every room has a kettle, a geyser in the washroom and mirrors.
  • The property is non-smoking throughout.
  • Free parking on site. There is road access to the property. Wheelchair and step-free access are not confirmed, so please ask us before you book.
  • Facilities include a garden, shared kitchen, shared lounge with TV area and a games room.
  • Heater electricity is ₹500 per day, or ₹3,000 per month for monthly guests, only if you use one.
  • Yoga and meditation sessions are not included in the room price.

Do’s and don’ts

  • Enjoy the house to the fullest, and responsibly.
  • Join in when the house does something together, but not at the expense of your work.
  • Say hello to the people sharing the house. A smile and a greeting never hurt.
  • Be kind and considerate to other guests.
  • Please do not smoke anywhere on the property.
  • Keep the noise low, especially in the shared lounge and work areas.

Mountain tips

  • Layers beat one big jacket. Evenings are cold even after a warm day.
  • Altitude: drink water, go slow on the first day.
  • Check weather and road status before trekking. Use licensed local guides for long routes.
