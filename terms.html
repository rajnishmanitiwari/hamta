Policy

Terms & conditions

Booking requests

A request on this website is not a booking. A booking exists when Hamta confirms availability and price to you and you pay in advance.

Rates and payment

Rates are in Indian rupees, per room, for up to two guests, and are the same for all guests. All taxes are included. Payment is made in advance, after we confirm availability and the final quote. Peak-date rates apply on national holidays, busy weekends, the last week of December, the first week of January and the first two weeks of February, and are confirmed before you pay.

Breakfast

Breakfast is included with daily stays, with or without the full meal package. Monthly stays without the full meal package do not include breakfast.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 12 PM. Check-out is at 11 AM the next day. You are welcome to arrive at any time of day, with no extra charge.

Monthly stays

A month is the calendar month, first to last date, or 30 days. Three rooms are kept for monthly stays.

Guests

Each room has a double bed for two guests. A third guest can be added on an extra mattress. Pets, children and unmarried couples are welcome with no limit. Smoking is not allowed. Alcohol is accepted.

If we cannot host you

If we are unable to provide your room or food, your payment is refunded in full, with no charge and no questions asked. See cancellation and refunds.

Contact

Questions: email hello@hamta.in or support@hamta.in, or use the contact page or WhatsApp +91 62398 15892.
