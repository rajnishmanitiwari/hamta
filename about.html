How to reach us

The Hamta Homestay, 15th Mile, Hallan-i, Manali, Himachal Pradesh 175136. Plus code 45V6+J9 Hallan-i. Follow the exact Google Maps pin.

Open in Google MapsDriving route from Delhi
  • By road. Delhi to Manali is about 530 to 540 km, 12 to 14 hours by car or bus.
  • By bus. Overnight buses run from Delhi to Manali. Confirm your drop-off point with Kabir before you board, and do not assume Manali town is the right stop.
  • By train. Manali has no railway station. Take a train to Chandigarh, about 300 km from Manali, then a bus or cab of roughly 7 to 9 hours.
  • By air. Fly to Kullu-Manali Airport at Bhuntar, about 50 km from Manali town. Taxis and buses are available.

Distances and times are for Delhi to Manali, not to the homestay, and are typical rather than guaranteed. Send Kabir your arrival time on WhatsApp and share your live location when you are close. Mountain roads and weather can slow you down, so plan some buffer.
