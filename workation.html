Questions

Workation, answered

What is a workation at The Hamta Homestay?

A workation is a 7 day, 7 night stay in Manali where you keep doing your work and reset how you live around it. It combines morning yoga and movement, structured deep work windows, a guided trek to Bijli Mahadev, complimentary river rafting, a complimentary one-year subscription to one of the most powerful personal AI agents, worth ₹40,000, evening unwind experiences and a closing clarity session, in a quiet mountain home.

Where does the workation take place?

At The Hamta Homestay, 15th Mile, Hallan-i, Manali, Himachal Pradesh 175136, India.

Who is the workation for?

Companies, organisations, teams, universities, remote professionals, founders, creators, freelancers and anyone who is building something and feeling stretched.

What is included?

A 7 day, 7 night mountain stay on double sharing (single sharing at additional cost), vegetarian meals throughout the stay, daily morning movement sessions, structured deep work windows, a guided trek to Bijli Mahadev, complimentary river rafting and a complimentary one-year subscription to one of the most powerful personal AI agents, worth ₹40,000, included in every package, curated evening unwind experiences and a final reset and clarity session.

Is there Wi-Fi and a place to work?

Yes. The homestay has Wi-Fi, constant electricity and a shared lounge with work tables. Hill internet can dip in bad weather, so keep a backup plan for critical calls.

How is workation pricing different from a normal stay?

Workation pricing is separate from standard nightly and monthly rates. Use the workation enquiry form and we reply with a plan and a price for your group.

How much does the workation cost?

The first week (7 days and 7 nights) is ₹14,999 per person for the Full reset experience or ₹12,999 per person for the Essential reset. If you continue for another week the next week is ₹10,999 or ₹9,499 per person. For a month or more after the first week it is ₹36,000 or ₹31,000 a month per person. Workation pricing is separate from our standard nightly and monthly rates, and we confirm the plan and price for your group.

Can we stay longer than a week?

Yes. You can continue for another week, or stay a month or more after your first week, at the extension prices listed on this page.

What happens on Saturday?

Day 7 is a free day with no plans. Guests explore on their own: nearby treks, waterfalls, lakes and places, camping and adventure, a bike ride to Sissu, local food and conversations with local people.

How do I enquire or book?

Send the workation enquiry form on this page or message us on WhatsApp. Nothing is booked or charged when you send an enquiry. Payment is made in advance once we confirm your dates.

What should I bring?

Two things: intentional work, such as a project or a goal, and openness to reset, meaning you are ready to slow down.
