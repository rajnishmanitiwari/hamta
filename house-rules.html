House rules

Welcome

  • Pets are welcome, with no limit.
  • Children are welcome, with no limit.
  • Unmarried couples are welcome.
  • Alcohol is accepted.

Not allowed

  • Smoking. The property is non-smoking throughout.

Good to know

  • Check-in from 12 PM, check-out at 11 AM. Arrive at any time.
  • Free parking on site. There is road access to the property. Wheelchair and step-free access are not confirmed, so please ask us before you book.
  • Free laundry with our washing machine.
  • Yoga and meditation sessions are not included in the room price.

Our home

This is a quiet mountain home. Please keep noise low and treat the space with care.
