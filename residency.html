Ten days, every quarter

The Hamta Residency

Ten days at Hamta, every quarter, almost entirely on us. You cover your food. We sponsor your stay.

Who it is for

Founders

People building something of their own, who get things done.

Creators

People making things worth sharing.

Community heads

People who bring others together.

We look for cool, talented, ambitious and driven people who can work on their own ideas, and who help Hamta grow while they are here.

How it works

Apply, get selected, come up

01

Apply

Tell us who you are and what you are working on.

02

Selection

We review every application and choose each cohort ourselves.

03

Ten days

Live and work at Hamta. Your stay and everything else is on us, food is on you.

04

Give back

Share your work, your skills and your ideas with the house, and help Hamta grow.

The deal

About 90% on the house.

  • A 10 day stay at The Hamta Homestay, every quarter
  • Your stay is sponsored by us
  • You cover only your own food
  • Everything else is on the house
  • Limited to selected applicants
